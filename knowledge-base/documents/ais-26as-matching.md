# AIS and Form 26AS Matching Rules

AIS shows income reported to the Income Tax Department from multiple sources. Form 26AS shows tax deducted and deposited against the user's PAN.

TaxAid should compare salary income, interest income, and TDS from AIS or 26AS with Form 16 and salary slips.

If AIS or 26AS is missing, TaxAid can still continue but should mark tax-paid verification as incomplete.
