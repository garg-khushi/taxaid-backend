# Salary Slip Extraction Rules

A salary slip usually contains monthly salary components such as Basic Salary, House Rent Allowance, Special Allowance, Performance Linked Pay, Provident Fund, Professional Tax, Total Earnings, Total Deductions, and Net Salary.

TaxAid should classify a document as a salary slip if it contains terms such as payslip, net salary, basic salary, house rent allowance, earnings, deductions, provident fund, or professional tax.

For private-sector employees, salary slips often include PF, professional tax, performance pay, bonus, or special allowance instead of government pay level and DA structure.
