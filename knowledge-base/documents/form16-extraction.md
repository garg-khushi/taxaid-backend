# Form 16 Extraction Rules

Form 16 is an employer-issued tax certificate. It usually contains employer details, employee PAN, assessment year, gross salary, exemptions, deductions, taxable income, and TDS deducted by employer.

TaxAid should use Form 16 to verify annual salary, TDS, employer TAN, deductions declared, and taxable income.

If Form 16 is missing, TaxAid should warn that TDS and taxable salary verification may be incomplete.
