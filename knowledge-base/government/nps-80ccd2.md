# Employer NPS and Section 80CCD(2)

Employer contribution to NPS may be deductible under Section 80CCD(2).

For government employees, employer NPS contribution can be a significant deduction and should be verified using Form 16. It is separate from 80C and 80CCD(1B).

TaxAid should flag this if:
- User is a government employee
- Basic Pay and DA are available
- NPS employer contribution is missing or not verified
