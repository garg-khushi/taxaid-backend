# DA Arrears and Section 89 / Form 10E

Government employees may receive DA arrears when DA is revised retrospectively.

A DA arrear can increase taxable salary in the year of receipt, even if it relates to an earlier period. In such cases, Section 89 relief may be relevant, and Form 10E may need to be reviewed before filing.

The user should confirm:
- Old DA rate
- New DA rate
- Effective month
- Payment month
- Number of arrear months
- Arrear amount
