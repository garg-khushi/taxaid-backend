# Government Employee HRA Rules

House Rent Allowance exemption is calculated using the least of three values:

1. Actual HRA received.
2. 50% of Basic Pay plus DA for metro cities, or 40% for non-metro cities.
3. Rent paid minus 10% of Basic Pay plus DA.

If rent proof is missing, the HRA claim may be flagged for review. Rent receipts, landlord details, and rent payment proof improve filing confidence.
