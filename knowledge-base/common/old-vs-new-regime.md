# Old Regime vs New Regime

The Old Regime may be better when the user has significant exemptions and deductions such as HRA, 80C, 80D, NPS, home loan interest, and profession-specific allowances.

The New Regime may be better when deductions are low and the user benefits more from lower slab rates.

TaxAid should compare both regimes using calculated taxable income, eligible deductions, exemptions, and expected tax payable.
