# Missing Proofs and Filing Risk

Missing proof can reduce filing confidence and increase review risk.

Common missing proofs:
- Rent receipts for HRA
- Medical insurance premium receipt for 80D
- NPS statement or Form 16 confirmation for 80CCD
- Home loan interest certificate
- Travel proof for LTC/LTA
- Form 10E review for arrears

TaxAid should explain missing proofs clearly and suggest what to upload next.
